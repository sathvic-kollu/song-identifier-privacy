Privacy Policy — Music ID: Song Identifier

Android app app.songidentifier.musicfinder · Publisher: Zoopet · Effective 13 October 2026

Music ID names the song playing near you. We built it so that it works without an account and keeps your song history on your phone. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles and why.

1. Microphone and song recognition

  • The app uses the microphone only after you tap the listen button (or the widget, Quick Settings tile or shortcut), for up to 15 seconds, while the app is open on screen. It never listens in the background.
  • The sound is turned into an audio signature on your phone. Only that signature is sent to Apple's ShazamKit service to find the song; the recording itself is not sent. Apple describes signatures as a one-way representation from which the original audio cannot be recreated.
  • If you are offline, a short clip (about 10 seconds) is saved in the app's private storage so it can be matched when you reconnect. It is deleted as soon as it is matched, or after 14 days. It is never backed up or shared.

2. Your song history

Identified songs, favourites and settings are stored only on your device. You can export them as a CSV file or delete them at any time in the app. If you use Android's own backup, your history and settings may be included in your personal Google backup; offline clips are excluded.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no push-notification SDK and no account system. We do not sell personal data.

4. Advertising and your choices

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) the app asks for your consent with Google's consent tool before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Buying "Remove ads" stops all ad requests.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone — song recognition, only when you start it.
  • Notifications (optional) — to tell you when songs saved offline have been identified.
  • Internet / network state — matching, catalogue, lyrics and ads.

6. Children

Music ID is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

7. Security and retention

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies: Apple, Deezer, Google, Unity, Cloudflare.

8. Lyrics

Lyrics come from the LRCLIB community. If you hold rights to lyrics shown in the app and want them removed, write to us and we will act promptly.
